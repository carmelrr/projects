Privacy Policy

TotemTV Video Processor is an internal automation tool used solely by the operator of the Totem climbing gym to process climbing-session video recordings and upload the processed files to a private Google Drive folder, for display on a TV screen inside the gym.

What it accesses

The tool connects only to the Google account and Drive folders it is explicitly configured for by its operator, in order to read submitted video files and write back processed output files.

What it does not do

It does not collect, sell, or share personal data with any third party. It is not a public product or service and is not intended for use by the general public.
